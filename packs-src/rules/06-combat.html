Combat and weapons

Range bands

Each weapon lists a power for each range band it can reach: Close (same zone), Medium (adjacent zone), Long (two zones or a clear line), Extreme (across the map, scoped rifles). An empty band is out of range. Each band away from the weapon's best band (marked *) adds +1 to the opposition.

Attacking

Target a token, then press the weapon's Attack button (crosshairs). The dialog asks for range, cover, whether the target is aware, aim and extra shots.

  1. Attack: 4dF + Shoot (Fight for melee, Throw for thrown weapons) + modifiers. Aiming gives +2 (you spend your move).
  2. Opposition: the target's Athletics roll if they are aware (Mediocre +0 if not), + range penalty + cover (light +1, heavy +2).
  3. Result: miss, near miss (Pinned) or hit.
  4. Damage on a hit = shifts + power (+1 per extra-shot +), then the ballistic die: + Vital (+2 damage and Bleeding), blank Clean, − Graze (−2, minimum 1). On a hit with style, roll it twice and keep the better.
  5. Apply: the target's owner (or the GM) presses Apply, picks consequences, and the module marks Health and conditions.

Extra shots and weapon stress

Depending on its action, a weapon can fire extra shots in the same attack: lever-action, pump-action, single-action revolvers and double barrels 1, double-action revolvers and semi-automatics 2 (traits such as Fanning and Levering raise it). Each extra shot rolls one Fate die: + adds 1 damage, blank is a missed round, − adds 1 weapon stress. When stress reaches the weapon's reliability, it jams: clear it with Crafts vs Fair (+2) (wrench button).

Reload fills the weapon from its spare rounds. An Ammo Box refills spare rounds.

Shapes: single, cone, area

Shotguns fire in a cone: everyone targeted in front of the shooter, in range. Area and cone attacks make one roll; each target defends with Athletics and gets its own Apply button; no ballistic die. Extra shots work the same (each + is +1 power for everyone hit).

Fate points on attacks

Every single-target attack card has a Fate point button: see the Fate points page.

Loadout

Only equipped weapons can fire (the toggle on their row in the Arsenal panel). Slots as in Hunt: Small 1, Medium 2, Large 3, a budget of 4 (5 with Quartermaster) and two weapons at most. A hunter also carries up to 4 tools (melee and thrown weapons, derringer, flare pistol, hand crossbow, kits, traps, beetles) and 4 consumables (explosives, shots, ammo boxes), outside the weapon slots.

Melee and thrown weapons

Melee weapons attack with Fight at Close, use no ammunition and never jam. Blunt weapons (dusters, hammer, shovel) never set off a volatile monster. Thrown weapons use Throw and are picked up again after the fight: Reload recovers them.
