Hunt: Showdown RPG

An unofficial tabletop adaptation of Hunt: Showdown for Foundry VTT, built on the Fate Core Official system. Free, made with the authorization of the rights holder. Hunters are hired to track a Bounty through the Stillwater Bayou, banish it and get out alive, while rival hunters want the same Bounty.

What is in this compendium

  • Hunt: Rules (this journal): the Fate basics you need, then every Hunt rule the module adds.
  • Hunt: Arsenal: handguns, long guns, grenades and bombs, thrown and melee weapons, support items.
  • Hunt: Bestiary: the Stillwater monsters, from Grunts to the five bosses.
  • Hunt: Traits: the three trait decks (Tier I, II, III).
  • Hunt: Macros: Start of Mission and End of Exchange.

The same arsenal, bestiary and decks are also created in the world (Items, Actors and Cards directories) when the rules are installed. The compendium is the reference copy: drag anything from it to restore or duplicate it.

Running a mission

  1. Start of Mission macro: every hunter's Fate points go back to their refresh (3).
  2. Hunters pick their loadout: two weapons within 4 slots, up to 4 tools and 4 consumables.
  3. Play scenes in zones. When a fight starts, put the tokens in the Combat Tracker: each round is one exchange.
  4. Advancing the round runs the End of Exchange: bleeding, poison, fire and regeneration are applied to every token on the scene.
  5. Clues, the Bounty, extraction: the GM's call, with Investigate, Lore and Notice.

Settings and menus

Game Settings → Configure Settings → Hunt: Showdown RPG: Hunt rules (install or reinstall), Hunt arsenal (create missing items or rebalance), Hunt traits (decks and hands), Hunt bestiary (create or rebuild), Monster Creator. The Actors directory has the Hunter Creator and, for the GM, the Monster Creator.
