Lore, keys and containers

Clippings, letters, photographs, toys, recordings and keys tell the story of the bayou. They are items: hunters find them in caches and containers, carry them (no slot, no limit), trade them and read them.

On a hunter's sheet, a lore item has a Read button. In the reader, Show to everyone opens it on every player's screen. The GM creates lore with the Lore Creator (Items directory) and edits it with the feather button; the Hunt: Lore compendium has a blank template of each kind and a set of Stillwater examples.

Locks

  • Doors: in a door wall's configuration, set a Lock ID and a pick difficulty, and set the door to Locked.
  • Containers (chest, desk, footlocker, safe): actors placed on the map as tokens, with a lock ID, a pick difficulty and a locked state. Drag lore and loot onto the container's sheet to fill it. Create them with the Lore Creator (Container) or from the Hunt: Containers compendium.

Searching and unlocking

  • Search (button on the hunter's loadout line): the targeted container within 2.5 m, or the nearest one, or the nearest locked door. If it is locked: use a matching key, or pick the lock (Burglary overcome vs the lock's difficulty, Fair +2 by default; a failure makes noise).
  • Key button on a key: opens the matching container or door within reach.
  • An open container lists its contents: Take one item or Take everything; items move to the hunter's sheet.

A GM must be connected: the GM's client unlocks doors and moves items for the players.
