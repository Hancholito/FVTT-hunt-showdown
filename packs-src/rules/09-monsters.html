Monsters

Monsters are Fate Core characters with monster data. Their attacks are natural attacks (no ammunition, no jams) fired with the same crosshairs button.

  • Category: Grunt, Elite or Boss.
  • Dread: the Will difficulty for a hunter it hits.
  • Armour: reduces every hit that isn't Vital.
  • Vital hit kills: a Vital hit (the head) kills it outright (Hive, Immolator).
  • Vital pierces armour: a Vital hit ignores its armour instead (Armored).
  • Volatile: a bullet, blade, arrow or blast that hits it without killing it sets it ablaze (Immolator): a fire burst (power 2, radius 4 m, Athletics vs Good +3) catches everyone around, its zone becomes Burning Ground, and each exchange it sets fire to anyone within 2 m. Blunt weapons, fire and smoke don't set it off.
  • Fireproof and immunities (Burning, Poisoned).
  • Phases (bosses): Idle, Attack, Rage, Pursuit — each with attack and defence bonuses, chosen on the sheet banner.

Monsters die when their last Health box is marked. Bosses also have consequences and 2 Fate points.

Monster Creator (Actors directory, GM): start blank or from a bestiary entry; skills, Health, armour, dread, options, up to three attacks and three abilities, and boss phases.
