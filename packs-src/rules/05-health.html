Health and harm

Health, chunks and Burned

A hunter has 6 Health boxes in three chunks of two (1–2, 3–4, 5–6). Each box absorbs 1 damage. Damage marks boxes from the last unburned chunk backward; healing clears them back from the front of the damage.

When a chunk is emptied by fire, or when a hunter is revived, the chunk burns away: mark a Burned box. Each Burned box strikes out two Health boxes for the rest of the mission. A Regeneration Shot restores one Burned chunk.

Consequences

When you take a hit, you can absorb part of it with a free consequence, named as a wound (Grazed Arm, Gut Shot): the Apply dialog asks which ones.

Recovery times are Fate's (a mission is a scenario). The Field Medic's Field Surgery downgrades a consequence by one step.

A consequence is an aspect: rivals and monsters can invoke it against you (and you earn the point at the end of the scene).

Downed and revived

Damage you can't absorb marks Downed: no action, no move, you can still talk. A teammate in your zone can revive you as their action: one chunk burns away. Monsters don't go down: they die when their last Health box is marked.

The module applies Pinned, Shaken, Poisoned and Blinded to attack and defence rolls automatically. Untick a condition on the sheet when it ends.

End of exchange

At the end of every exchange — automatically when the Combat Tracker moves to the next round, or with the End of Exchange macro — every token on the scene takes its ongoing damage: Bleeding 1, Heavy Bleeding 2, Poisoned 1, Burning 1 box (and the chunk it empties burns). Ordinary monsters that are Burning die; bosses, fireproof monsters and the Immolator don't. A Regeneration Shot clears 1 box per exchange for 3 exchanges (not while Poisoned). One chat card sums it all up. Downed hunters and dead monsters are skipped.

Dread

When a monster's attack hits a hunter, the hunter rolls Will against the monster's dread. On a failure, mark a Dread box; the third one marks Shaken. Dread clears with a Will roll after the skirmish.
