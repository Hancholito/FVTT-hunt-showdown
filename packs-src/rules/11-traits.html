Traits

Traits are cards, in three decks by cost: Tier I (cost 1–3, found in marked caches), Tier II (4–5, taken from elite monsters) and Tier III (6–8, won from the Bounty). A hunter starts with one trait (the Hunter Creator deals it) and finds the others in play.

  • Each player has a hand of 4 traits at most. Drawing into a full hand is refused: discard one first.
  • To use a trait, play it to Played Traits: its effect is posted to chat and the card stays in your hand. Active traits (red band) cost 1 Fate point; triggered traits cost nothing.
  • To drop a trait, pass it to Discarded Traits.
