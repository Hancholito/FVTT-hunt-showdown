Explosives and fire

Explosives are single use: once thrown, the item leaves the sheet (a Fusee spends one of its three charges).

  1. Throw (bomb button): choose the zone — same zone Average (+1), adjacent Fair (+2), two zones Great (+4, needs Pitcher). On a failure, the scatter die says where it lands: + one zone beyond, blank short, − in the thrower's own zone. A sticky bomb is thrown at one targeted token instead (Throw vs their Athletics): whoever it sticks to gets no defence.
  2. Detonate (button on the throw card): click the impact point on the map. Every token within the blast radius is caught, friend or foe, the thrower included; the outer half of the radius takes −2 power. Each victim defends with Athletics against the throw total (Fair +2 if the throw failed) and gets its own Apply button. Dead monsters are never caught again.

It goes off at the end of the exchange, unless a Sapper cooks the fuse (at once). Damage = shifts + power. Power 0 explosives (Flash, Poison, Choke, Chaos, Hive) do no damage: whoever is caught only gets the conditions and the zone aspect.

Fire

Any fire hit — fire bombs, incendiary ammo, anything that inflicts Burning — kills ordinary monsters outright. Bosses, fireproof monsters (the Meathead) and monsters immune to Burning (the Immolator) take the damage normally. On hunters, Burning burns a Health box every exchange and the chunk it empties burns away: put it out.
