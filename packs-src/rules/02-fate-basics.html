Fate Core in five minutes

Hunt runs on Fate Core. This page sums up the parts of Fate the module relies on. The complete rules are free online in the Fate Core System Reference Document; for how the character sheets and dice work in Foundry, see the @UUID[Compendium.fate-core-official.documentation.JournalEntry.0nYbHOc3AErjhSUJ]{Fate Core Official documentation}.

Dice and the ladder

Every roll is 4dF (four Fate dice: −, blank, +), from −4 to +4, added to a skill. Results are read on the ladder:

+8Legendary+3Good
+7Epic+2Fair
+6Fantastic+1Average
+5Superb0Mediocre
+4Great−1/−2Poor / Terrible

The four outcomes

Compare your total to the opposition (a fixed difficulty or someone else's roll). The difference is the shifts.

  • Fail (below): you don't get it, or you get it at a serious cost.
  • Tie (equal): you get it at a minor cost, or a lesser version. In Hunt, a tied attack is a near miss: the target is Pinned.
  • Succeed (1–2 shifts): you get it.
  • Succeed with style (3+ shifts): you get it and something extra. In Hunt, a hit with style rolls the ballistic die twice and keeps the better.

The four actions

  • Overcome: get past an obstacle (pick a lock, cross the mud, silence a dog).
  • Create an advantage: create or discover an aspect, and get a free invoke on it (two with style). Flanked, Smoke Cover, Spotted his Scope Glint.
  • Attack: harm someone. In Hunt, use the weapon buttons: the module handles range, cover, defence, ballistic die and damage.
  • Defend: oppose an attack or an advantage. Against weapons, Athletics (the module rolls it).

Aspects

Aspects are short phrases that are true in the fiction: a hunter's High Concept and Trouble, a zone's Burning Ground, a wound's consequence. They do three things:

  • Invoke: spend a Fate point (or a free invoke) and explain how the aspect helps: +2 to your roll, or reroll the four dice. You can also invoke an aspect on someone else, for or against them.
  • Compel: the aspect complicates your life. Accept and you earn a Fate point; refuse and you pay one.
  • Establish facts: Choking Smoke means nobody sees through it, no roll needed.

A boost is a fleeting aspect with one free invoke that vanishes once used.

Skills and stunts

Skills are rated on the ladder; a hunter's skills form a pyramid (one Great, two Good, three Fair, four Average). Stunts are special abilities that bend the rules for a skill: each hunter starts with one free stunt, their archetype's signature.

Conflicts

A fight is a conflict, played in exchanges: everyone acts once per exchange, in turn order. The scene is split into zones (a room, a stretch of road, a barn loft): moving one zone is free with your action, more needs an Athletics roll or your whole action. Harm is absorbed by your Health boxes and consequences (see Health and harm). Anyone can concede before the dice are rolled: they leave the conflict on their own terms and earn Fate points (see Fate points). If you can't absorb a hit, you are taken out: in Hunt, Downed.
